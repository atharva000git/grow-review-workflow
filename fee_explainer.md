# Brokerage Explainer (Support Snippet)

- Equity brokerage is the lower of ₹20 or 0.1% per executed order.
- A ₹5 minimum equity brokerage applies per order.
- F&O brokerage is fixed at ₹20 per executed order.
- Separate orders incur separate brokerage charges.
